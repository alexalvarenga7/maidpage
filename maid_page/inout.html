Moving In/Out Cleaning

Moving out can be stressful, but cleaning doesn’t have to be. Our Move Out Cleaning service ensures your old home is spotless and ready for its next chapter.

General Cleaning

  • Ceiling Fan(s) (within reach)
  • Air Vents
  • Light Fixtures
  • Outlet Covers / Switch Plates
  • Wall Scuffs (within reach)
  • Windows: all glass, framing, and ledges
  • Dust blinds
  • Doors: all surfaces, frames, and knobs
  • Closets: door tracks, shelving, and floors
  • Scrub baseboards
  • Sweep, vacuum (if carpet), and mop all floors

Bathroom(s) General

  • All Cabinets: interior and exterior (within reach)
  • All Drawers: interior and exterior
  • Sink
  • Faucet
  • Counter
  • Toilet
  • Exhaust Vent

Bathtub / Shower

  • Enclosure / Surround Tiles / Fixtures
  • Shower Rod / Door
  • Faucets / Controls

Kitchen General

  • All Cabinets: interior and exterior (within reach)
  • All Drawers: interior and exterior
  • Stovetop / Splash Guards / Backsplash
  • Sink
  • Faucet
  • Counter(s)

Fridge Cleaning Checklist

  • Full Interior: kickplate, shelving, bars
  • Remove & clean ice tray from freezer
  • Exterior clean: sides, above (within reach)
  • Polish steel (if applicable)

Microwave

  • Interior
  • Exterior clean
  • Polish steel (if applicable)

Oven / Range

  • Full inside clean
  • Broiler / Drawer
  • Drip Pans
  • Exterior clean
  • Polish steel (if applicable)

Dishwasher

  • Tub interior / Soap dispenser
  • Exterior controls / Exterior surface
  • Polish steel (if applicable)

Exterior (if applicable)

  • Sweep balcony / patio / garage
  • Sweep storage area

Laundry Room (if applicable)

  • Washer: interior tub and lid
  • Dryer: interior tub, lid, and lint trap

Before Leaving

  • Close all blinds
  • Adjust thermostat to the temperature upon entry
  • Turn off all lights
  • Walk through unit to confirm task completion
  • Lock all doors and windows
  • Take out trash (2 bags included)

  • Mold and Mildew Removal
  • Cleaning Human or Animal Bodily Fluids
  • Cleaning Exterior Windows, Power Washing Exterior
  • Disposal of Large Items or Excessive Trash
  • Cleaning Fireplaces
